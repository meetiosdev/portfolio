LandBurro is designed to connect people who want to rent their property with people who want to rent it - short term rentals for land, experiences and lodging. While anyone can enjoy public lands, having access to a private piece of land for your own solitary enjoyment offers a different type of joy!

- Easily switch between map view and list view in the search results
- Save properties in your favorites

- Purchase Gift Cards
- LandHosts are able to blackout dates that property will not be available.
- Earn BurroBucks (our rewards program) through completing bookings.
- Access and edit your account information
- See past trips and future trips
- Share properties and trips with friends

The Great Outdoors is at your fingertips and happiness awaits! Download our app and go explore! Experience life.
